# Quick-commerce market notes (internal, sample data)

## Our position
FreshBasket (our company) operates dark stores in 6 Indian cities with an average delivery time of 14 minutes.
Average order value is INR 540; repeat customers place 4.2 orders per month.

## Competitor watch
Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart dominate metro markets. Our internal estimate is that the top three hold
about 85% of quick-commerce order volume in the six cities we serve.

## Risks we track
Dark-store rent is rising roughly 12% a year. Rider availability drops during monsoon months.
Regulators are reviewing deep-discounting by quick-commerce platforms.

## Strategy 2026
Expand to 4 tier-2 cities, add a private-label staples range, and keep delivery under 15 minutes.
